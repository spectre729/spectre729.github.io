The story

Most projects that I've worked on was for a class. This one wasn't. As a (mostly) free-to-play, or F2P, War Robots players, I wanted to help other F2P players in increasing their chance of obtaining the best equipments in war robots. Keeping track of giveaway videos across a few big YouTubers is tiring, and keeping track of every eligible YouTubers would be worse. Oddly enough, I keet missing good giveaways buried in a flood of low-effort YouTube videos, and I figured I could filter the noise better than scrolling ever would.

It's grown into the first thing I've built that real people actually use without me asking them to: it's running across dozens of Discord servers, quietly doing its job every day. That made it a different kind of project to work on — no spec, no deadline, just "does this actually work for the people using it."

How it works

A scheduled job pulls candidate videos from YouTube for a set of tracked creators and search terms. Each candidate goes through a two-stage filter — first a cheap keyword pass, then an LLM classification step for anything ambiguous — before it's checked against a Postgres table of giveaways already seen. What survives gets pushed out to subscribed Discord servers.

The whole thing runs on a small Google Compute Engine instance, with the pipeline built around a simple principle: do the cheap filtering first, and only spend an LLM call on videos that actually need judgment.
