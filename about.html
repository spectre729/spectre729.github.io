About

The longer version

Hello there! I'm a Computer Science student at Cornell University as a Master of Engineering candidate, where I will graduate in December 2026. I like systems that involves a lot of moving parts, such as queues, retries, async pipelines, but I'm also curious how about these systems talk with the frontend. Lately, I've been working on ways on I can add LLMs that significantly improves my workflow.

In addition to my coursework, I'm also juggling between two internships: I'm an AI Engineer Intern at Redtail Studios, where I work on the async infrastructure behind a market intelligence platform, and a Full-Stack Developer Intern at Altheros Capital, where I bounce between Swift, backend APIs, and shipping web pages for the firm and its portfolio companies, including Insane Vegan,which I'm moving into a tech lead role. Although if I'm being totally honest, they're both Software Engineer roles with a fancy-ish titles.

Before that, I spent the 2025-2026 academic year as a Machine Learning Researcher at Gao Labs, working on adaptive sampling models for real-world uncertainty under the supervision of Yishuo Jiang and leading a small group of engineers turning research code into reproducible training pipelines. This semester, I've shifted gear and worked on world models.

Additionally, I've also been a teaching assistant at Cornell since fall 2023. I actually started off as an undergrad consultant, and got promoted to undergrad teaching assistant in spring 2026, and gradaute teaching assistant this semester.

Outside of work and school, I built and maintain wr-giveaway-finder, a Discord bot that finds War Robots giveaways on YouTube and shares them to various discord server(include Sattu and Universe Pro), that's grown actual users across dozens of servers. Funny enough, it's my first project that wasn't just for a grade or an employer, which made it a different kind of learning experience.
